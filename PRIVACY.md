# Privacy Policy — PodTrend Analytics

PodTrend Analytics is an internal, personal-use analytics and research tool.

- **Data Usage:** It accesses public trend metrics and keyword growth data solely for internal e-commerce product planning and design research.
- **No Redistribution:** No personal user data is collected, stored, sold, or shared with third parties.
- **Contact:** Managed internally via `https://github.com/abdcaliskan/PodTrend-`.
